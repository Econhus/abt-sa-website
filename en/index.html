About

Integrated Contracting.
Clear Accountability.

Aamar Al-Bayan Al-Thabit Contracting (ABT) is a Saudi-based general contracting firm, established to be a trusted partner in construction and electromechanical engineering across the Kingdom.

We deliver comprehensive solutions across every project phase — from requirements study, development, and mobilization to execution, testing, commissioning, and maintenance — coordinating site, supply, and quality teams under a single track.
